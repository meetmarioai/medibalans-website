2. What data we process

Identity and contact data: name, email, phone number, and information you provide when booking and contacting us.

Sensitive personal data (special categories under GDPR Art. 9) — health data:

  • Laboratory results: ALCAT (food reactivity), CMA (cellular micronutrient analysis), WGS/genomic data (whole-genome sequencing)
  • Symptoms, dietary information, clinical notes and protocol data
  • Your conversation with Ask Mario (the AI-based clinical assistant)

Genomic data is the most sensitive category of personal data we process. It is processed solely within the scope of your care and analysis.

Payment and booking data: handled via our payment and booking providers (see section 6).

Technical and analytics data: when you visit the website, cookies and analytics tools may collect technical information (see section 7).

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data (subject access request)
  • request rectification of inaccurate data
  • request erasure ("the right to be forgotten"), within the limits of statutory record-keeping obligations
  • request restriction of processing
  • object to processing
  • obtain your data (data portability)
  • withdraw consent you have given

Contact us at info@medibalans.com to exercise your rights.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority: the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), Box 8114, 104 20 Stockholm, imy@imy.se.
